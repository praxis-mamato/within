# Stage 1 interview guide

For the 15 to 20 Stage 1 interviews (PRD §11). About 30 minutes each. Use a phone, or a laptop narrowed to phone width.

## Before the participant arrives

1. Open the app with `?interview` at the end of the address (for example `https://praxis-mamato.github.io/within/?interview`).
2. Pick the lens condition: **Both**, **Western only**, or **Vedic only**. Rotate it so each condition gets roughly a third of sessions, and note which participants know Western, Vedic, both, or little astrology.
3. Tap **Start a fresh session**. This erases anything left on the device. A bar at the top shows the session code and elapsed time.

## During the session

- Say: "This is an early version. We're testing the app, not you. Please think out loud."
- Let them complete setup on their own. Only step in if they're stuck for more than a minute, and note it.
- Real birth details are fine: they stay on the device and are erased at the end. Sample details are fine too.
- If anything they say or type suggests fear, coercion, or danger, stop the task, follow the safety runbook, and don't continue the session.

## After the first reflection

The app moves to **Three quick questions**. Ask each one aloud and rate the spoken answer:

| Question | Accurate means |
|---|---|
| What is this reflection saying? | A fair summary in their own words |
| Is it a fact about you, or an interpretation? | "An interpretation" (or equivalent) |
| What would you do next, if anything? | Names a step, a pause, or nothing, and knows it's their choice |

Then tap **See the summary**, then **Copy summary**, and paste it into the study notes with the participant's study ID (never their name).

## Wrap-up questions (your own notes)

- What, if anything, was useful?
- Was anything confusing, worrying, or uncomfortable?
- (Both lenses) Did having two traditions help, or make it harder?
- Would you come back next week? What would bring you back?
- Would you pay $9.99 a month for this? Why or why not?

## Afterwards

Tap **End and erase this session** so nothing stays on the device.
