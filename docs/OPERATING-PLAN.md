# Within operating plan

Maggie, Oct 30: "Build our business like you have skin in the game, and drive us to be better and
stable at the same time." Claude runs the business day to day against this plan. Every agent (growth
lead, social manager, deal-maker, admin bot) works toward it.

## Where we are (Oct 30)
- 1 paying member ($9.99/month), 3 comp members, 7 accounts. Target for Nov 12 was 6 paying: behind.
- People who try the Oracle use it (about 12 a week ask questions). The constraint is **reach**: too few
  people find Within. Conversion is second.

## Bets, in order
1. **Reach: search.** 166 pages live (planets in signs, rising, nakshatras, lunations, astrology today,
   gift). Next: "vs" comparison pages, daily horoscopes per sign, Google auto-resubmit (needs a key).
2. **Reach: social.** Daily reel, feed post and three stories on Instagram, timed to the sky. Next:
   Threads, Facebook, Pinterest and YouTube Shorts as accounts open; numbers reported daily.
3. **Revenue: holiday gifts.** Gift a year ($100), live Oct 30. Promote from Nov 1 through Dec 24.
4. **Reach: partners.** 20+ shops and studios on 30% affiliate. Sending needs a human (Instagram blocks
   bots from first DMs): Maggie, or email from the business address.
5. **Conversion.** Make the first answer feel unmistakably personal, then the paywall. Measure with
   opt-in events plus Stripe.
6. **Store apps.** iPhone (waiting on Apple account), Microsoft Store, Android (waiting on D-U-N-S).

## Stability: what stays true every day
- Admin bot checks health every morning (functions, payments, bookings, codes, errors) and fixes what it
  safely can.
- Tests must pass before every deploy (`npm test`, `npm run build`); no change ships untested.
- AI spend is capped ($10/day) with per-person limits; Instagram auth errors keep posts queued, not lost.
- Facts are computed, never guessed: positions match Swiss Ephemeris; content lint blocks fear,
  predictions and banned words.
- No birth data on servers. No personal names in public. No first DMs.

## Decision rights
- **Claude decides and ships:** content, social, SEO, product and conversion changes, bug fixes, new
  pages, partner drafts, anything reversible that costs no money.
- **Maggie decides:** spending money, prices and discounts, legal and account ownership, anything said
  in her own name, live readings.

## Rhythm
- Daily: social manager posts and replies; admin bot health line (silent if fine).
- Monday: growth lead scorecard (target vs actual vs forecast) and the yes/no list.
- Maggie hears from Claude when there is a result, a decision, or a problem. Not for routine.
