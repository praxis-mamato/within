## 2. Diagnosis: the weakest step to a payment

**Between "Sign in" and "Subscribe" there is an extra email step.** On the web paywall a visitor must
(1) sign in with Google, then (2) press "Email me a link", open their inbox, and click the link on the
same device, and only then (3) see the Subscribe button. The checkout server does not require this step
(`supabase/functions/create-checkout/index.ts` only checks sign-in); it is a client-side "confirm this
device" check. Each extra step before a payment typically loses a large share of people, and inbox
round-trips on a phone are the worst kind.

Evidence: 2 sign-ins, 1 checkout started, 0 paid. Too few to prove, but the structure alone puts us at
the low end of the conversion range. **Recommendation:** let signed-in people subscribe directly, and keep
the email confirmation for the account and device-sensitive actions only. Needs your yes (sign-in /
payment flow).

Second weakest: **running out of free questions was a dead end** (a line of text, no button). Fixed today.
