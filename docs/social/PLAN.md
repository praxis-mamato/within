# @withintheoracle: the daily plan for views

Built from current research (Oct 2026; sources in docs/social/research-2026-10.md). The short version of how Instagram ranks things now:
- **Watch time, likes, and sends** are the top signals (Mosseri, Jan 2025). **Sends** matter most for reaching people who don't follow us yet.
- Every original post gets a test audience of non-followers; how they react decides whether it spreads to Explore and the Reels tab.
- **Max 5 hashtags.** Search reads the caption (first 125 characters), the text on screen, and spoken words, so keywords go there.
- "Comment YES" and "tag a friend" are treated as engagement bait and can cut reach. **"Send this to your Scorpio friend" is fine** and feeds the strongest signal.
- Original only: no reposts, no watermarks. Raw and real beats polished.
- Best US windows: **Tue–Thu, about noon–2pm ET**; Mon/Tue afternoons next; weekends and Fridays weaker.

## Every post
- **Hook in the first second**, in big text on screen ("Scorpio, this is your sign to…").
- **Captions burned in** (most people watch muted). Original audio only: the API can't add music. Maggie may add a trending sound by hand to the week's best reel.
- **Caption:** keyword-first line (e.g. "Scorpio horoscope for the Full Moon in Taurus"), 2–4 short lines, then **"Send this to your ___ friend ✦"** or "Ask the Oracle, link in bio", then 3–5 specific hashtags.
- **The crystal ball** is the visual signature on everything.

## What we measure (weekly, in docs/social/report-<date>.md)
- Sends per reach, average watch time, saves, follows from each post, and app visits from the bio link.
- After 3 weeks: keep the two best formats, replace the weakest, and move posting times to our own best hours.

## Honest expectations
There is no reliable data on how fast a new astrology account grows; reach is falling across Instagram, and growth tends to come from a few breakout reels rather than steady gains. Daily original reels give us the most chances at a breakout. Once the account qualifies, Trial Reels (shown to non-followers first) are the safest way to test formats.
