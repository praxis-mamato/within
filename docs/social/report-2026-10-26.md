## What I change
1. **More sign-by-sign reels, fewer plain pause reels.** Three "pause on your sign / house" reels a week (Monday, plus the two biggest sky events), one explainer, one pick, one send-to-a-friend. Pause-the-ball reels drop to one a week.
2. **Hook in the first second.** Average watch time of 2–3 seconds means most people leave before the payoff. Open each reel on the answer's setup in big text ("Leo: your 10th house lights up") and put the first reveal inside the first second, not after the intro.
3. **Sound:** voice recordings (Maggie's friend) on the best 2–3 reels a week once the files arrive; trending audio by hand on the week's best reel.
4. **Feed images and stories stay as a daily minimum** (Maggie's rule) but get less effort; the effort goes into reels and one carousel a week.
5. **Growth outside the content** (only Maggie can do these today): fill the bio, pin three posts, collab posts with the deal-maker's partner list, poll stickers on stories, and daily follows and comments from the account by hand (drafts on request).
