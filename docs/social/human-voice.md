## How we write now
1. **Start where the person is.** Open with something they have felt or done, like reading their horoscope twice or being 28 when everything suddenly needs a serious look. Don't open with a definition or a product line.
2. **Write to one person.** Use "you" and contractions, and write it the way you'd text a friend who reads charts. The reader stays "the reader", with no name.
3. **One idea per post.** Don't list three features. Mention "Western and Vedic" only when the post is about the difference.
4. **Keep the facts exact.** Keep the real degrees, dates and prices. Being specific is what makes a post sound like a person who knows astrology, not a brand.
5. **Cut the AI tells.** Drop the ✦ in the first line, the ◆ bullet lists (except a real reference list), "not X, it's Y" lines, piles of adjectives, and lines like "the energy is shifting".
6. **End with an invitation.** Use a real question or "Ask the Oracle, link in bio." Don't add both a sales line and a share line every time.
7. **The content lint still applies.** That means no "will", "always", "never", "destiny" or "soulmate", and no predictions.
