Privacy policy

Effective November 17, 2026

The short version. Your chart is calculated on your device. Your birth details, journal and notes stay on your device. When Within writes an answer with AI, it sends calculated chart facts, never your name or birth details. We don't sell data, show ads or use tracking pixels.

Who we are

Within: The Oracle ("Within", "we") is the astrology app at withinoracle.app. Questions about this policy or your data: privacy@praxisglobal.ai.

What stays on your device

Your birth date, time and place, your chart, journal, milestones, reflections, the people you add, and (if you turn it on) the Oracle's memory of past conversations. These are saved, encrypted, in your browser or app on your device. We can't see them. You can export them or erase them in Settings.

What we keep on our servers

  • Your account: if you sign in, your email address and sign-in details from Google.
  • Membership: whether you're a member, your plan and its dates, and any gift or access code you used. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Usage limits: counts of questions and readings, so we can keep free limits and costs in check. For people who aren't signed in, we use a daily hash of the network address, never the address itself. We don't store what you asked or what the Oracle answered.
  • Live readings: if you book an astrologer, your booking, your questions and availability, and your birth details only if you tick the box to share them with the astrologer.
  • Reviews: a review you choose to send. It is shown publicly only if you agree and we approve it.
  • Optional sharing: anonymous usage counts and crash reports, only if you turn on "Help improve Within." These hold fixed categories and numbers, never free text.

AI answers

The Oracle's answers, deep readings and conversations are written by Claude, an AI model from Anthropic. For each one, your device sends your question, your calculated chart facts and Within's reviewed interpretations. It never sends your name, birth details, place, journal or relationship notes. Anthropic processes this to write the answer under its commercial terms and does not use it to train models. Deep readings can be turned off in Settings.

Within in Claude

If you connect Within to your own Claude account, you approve it on a Within consent screen. Claude sends the birth details you give it so Within can calculate the chart. These are used for that calculation and are not stored. Within checks your membership to decide which readings you get. Your conversation stays in your Claude account under Anthropic's terms. You can disconnect anytime in Claude's connector settings.

Services we use

  • Supabase: accounts, membership records and server functions.
  • Cloudflare: hosting the website and the Claude connector.
  • Stripe: payments.
  • Anthropic (Claude): writing AI answers, as described above.
  • Google: sign-in, and the web fonts the site loads (which means Google sees your network address when the page loads).

We don't sell or rent personal data, and we don't use advertising or tracking pixels.

How long we keep it

Account and membership records are kept while you have an account. Usage counts are kept as long as needed for limits and billing records. Payment records are kept as the law requires. Deleting your account removes your account and membership records and cancels any subscription.

Your choices and rights

  • Export or erase everything on your device in Settings.
  • Delete your account in Settings, or email us.
  • Ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, at privacy@praxisglobal.ai. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you have these rights by law, and we honor them for everyone.

Children

Within isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. If a change is significant, we'll say so in the app.
